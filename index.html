Our Pillars

Where resilience takes root

Showing up with kindness, day after day, takes capacity. We help people build it in body, heart, and mind, so there's something to draw on when life gets hard.

Body

Warmth, water, rest, and nourishment. Caring for the body lets the nervous system settle and restores the patience and energy that kindness draws on.

Why Kindness

Kindness is a capacity, not a trait

Research points the same way again and again: whether people act kindly depends a lot on the conditions they're living in. That's why we invest in conditions, not just good intentions.

63% → 10%

Hurry crowds out help

In a classic study, seminary students walking to give a talk passed a stranger slumped in a doorway. 63% stopped to help when they had time. Only 10% did when they were rushed.

Darley & Batson, 1973 · Stanford SPARQ summary
1 in 2

Many of us are running on empty

About half of U.S. adults report experiencing loneliness, according to the U.S. Surgeon General's 2023 advisory, which names social connection as essential to health.

U.S. Surgeon General, 2023 · AHRQ summary
3 degrees

Kindness spreads

In network experiments, one person's cooperative act influenced others up to three degrees of separation away, including people who never met the original giver.

Fowler & Christakis, 2010 · PNAS
27 studies

Giving gives back

A review of 27 studies found that performing acts of kindness gives a small but real boost to the well-being of the person being kind. Kindness helps refill the capacity it draws on.

Curry et al., 2018 · Oxford Research Archive

Care builds capacity. Capacity makes kindness possible. Kindness, shared, makes a kind city.

About Kind City

Kindness that holds up under pressure

Anyone can be kind on a good day. The real test comes when people are exhausted, isolated, or afraid, and that's exactly when communities need kindness most. Kind City builds the everyday strength that makes kindness possible: bodies that are cared for, hearts that feel connected, and minds that can stay steady under stress.

We're building in phases, starting with what we can launch now. Kind City Baths and Kind City Rock Camp lead the way, with Mind programming to follow. We're building in the open, so the city can grow with us instead of waiting for a finished plan.
