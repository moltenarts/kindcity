Our Pillars

Three pillars, one city

Every program we support falls under one of three pillars, each addressing a different part of what it means to live well.

Bodies

Programs that nourish physical health and wellbeing: wellness, hygiene, and food, helping people meet basic needs and feel cared for.

Flagship Program Kind City Baths Coming Soon

Hearts

Music and arts programs that build confidence, connection, and belonging, especially for young people whose voices go unheard.

Flagship Program Kind City Rock Camp Coming Soon

Minds

Programs in meditation and somatic therapies that help people process stress, feel at home in their bodies, and build lasting mental wellbeing.

About Kind City Trust

A kinder city is built on purpose

Kindness isn't a personality trait. It's a capacity that communities build or lose, depending on whether their members feel cared for. Kind City Trust exists to build that capacity by meeting real needs across body, heart, and mind, so kindness can become not just an aspiration, but a practice.

We're just getting started. Our flagship programs are in development now, and we're building in the open, so the city can grow with us.
